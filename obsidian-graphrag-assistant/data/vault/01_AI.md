# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broad field of building systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding natural language, recognising patterns, making decisions, and generating content.

## Subfields

AI encompasses many subfields, including machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing (NLP), and robotics. The most relevant to this knowledge base is NLP, where [[LLM|Large Language Models]] have become the dominant approach.

## Modern AI Capabilities

Modern AI systems can:
- Generate human-quality text and code
- Understand and reason over large amounts of information
- Retrieve and synthesise knowledge from diverse sources

## Key Related Concepts

[[LLM]] is the core technology enabling modern conversational AI.

[[RAG]] combines AI generation with external knowledge retrieval.

[[Knowledge_Graph]] provides structured representation of AI knowledge.

[[GraphRAG]] is an advanced AI retrieval pattern that fuses graphs with language models.

## Limitations

Current AI systems are limited by their [[Context_Window]], which restricts how much information can be processed at once. This is one motivation for retrieval-augmented approaches.
